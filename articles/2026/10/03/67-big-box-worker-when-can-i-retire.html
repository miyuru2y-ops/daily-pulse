67, earning $19.50 an hour: when can this big-box worker finally retire?

October 03, 2026 · 1 min read · Source: MarketWatch

At 67, working a big-box store job for $19.50 an hour, one reader says they don’t want to ‘die on the sales floor’ — and wants to know when retirement is realistic.

They started taking Social Security at 66 and now receive $2,410 a month, with $214,000 saved in a 401(k).

The question, put to MarketWatch’s columnist, is how those pieces fit together — and how soon they can afford to stop working.
