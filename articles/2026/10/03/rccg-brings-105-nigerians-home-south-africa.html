RCCG brings 105 stranded Nigerians home from South Africa amid xenophobia fears

October 03, 2026 · 1 min read · Source: TheNewsGuru

Another 105 Nigerians stranded in South Africa have returned home through a repatriation initiative sponsored by the Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG).

The group, made up of 55 adults, 45 children and five infants, arrived at the Murtala Muhammed International Airport in Lagos late on Friday.

The evacuation comes amid growing concern over anti-migrant hostility in South Africa, with reports of threats against foreign nationals prompting fears of xenophobic violence.

The church-sponsored flights are the latest in a series of repatriation efforts aimed at bringing stranded Nigerians home safely.
