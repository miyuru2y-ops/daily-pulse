Red Wings fans boo Dylan Larkin in frustrated season opener

October 03, 2026 · 1 min read · Source: Google News Sports

Detroit Red Wings fans made their frustration known during pregame introductions, loudly booing captain Dylan Larkin before a loss.

The jeers came amid reports surrounding a trade request, which the team’s head coach addressed afterward, saying he felt bad for the player.

Larkin has since been placed on injured reserve, compounding a difficult start for Detroit.

Teammate Moritz Seider also addressed the booing — and the ‘sell the team’ chants ringing around the arena — as the Red Wings opened their 101st season with supporters venting mounting frustration.
