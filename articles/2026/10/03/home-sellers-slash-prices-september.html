One in three home sellers cut prices in September as buyers stay hesitant

October 03, 2026 · 1 min read · Source: MarketWatch

Roughly one in three home sellers slashed their asking price in September in a bid to attract reluctant buyers, new data shows.

Hesitant buyers have been driving home prices down, and sellers are increasingly having to meet the market with cuts.

Some areas saw the highest share of price reductions and the largest markdowns, underscoring how uneven the slowdown has become.
