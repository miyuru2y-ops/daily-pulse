Leaked European prices suggest steep hikes for Oppo Find X10 series

October 03, 2026 · 1 min read · Source: GSMArena.com

Leaked European pricing for the Oppo Find X10 and Find X10 Pro Max suggests buyers could be in for a shock when the phones go on sale.

Smartphone prices have been climbing as memory and storage chips now cost many times more than they did a few years ago, largely because of surging demand from AI data centers.

Even against that backdrop, the leaked figures for Oppo’s upcoming flagships stand out as particularly steep, according to the report.

Oppo has not officially confirmed pricing for the Find X10 series.
