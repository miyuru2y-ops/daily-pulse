Catherine Connolly faces awkward duty of welcoming Trump to Áras an Uachtaráin

Just now  |  World  |  1 min read

Image: Independent.ie

Catherine Connolly has never hidden her opinion of Donald Trump. She has publicly described the US president as volatile, a bully and a misogynist — words that now hang over what promises to be one of the most awkward diplomatic encounters in recent Irish history.

In her official capacity, Connolly is required to formally welcome Trump to Áras an Uachtaráin, the official residence of the Irish president, setting up a meeting loaded with political tension.

The encounter was the subject of discussion on Independent.ie’s podcast, where host Kevin Doyle was joined by guests Mary Regan and John Downing to examine what could possibly go wrong when two such publicly opposed figures meet face to face.

The meeting underscores the delicate balance elected officials must strike between personal conviction and diplomatic protocol.
